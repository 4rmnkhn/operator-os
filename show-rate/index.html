the show-rate sequence

the single biggest lever on a call funnel. a booked call costs you content, dms and a setter's hours. a no-show burns all three. this is the exact message spine i hold setters to a 70% show rate with. five touches, written out, yours.

1 the five touches 2 when they don't show 3 the booking flow behind it
1

the five touches

every message does one job: make the call feel real, scheduled and worth protecting. the working-call framing matters most: people skip pitches. they show up to work sessions about their own numbers.

instantly on booking · email locked: [day], [time] [their timezone]. calendar file's attached, tap it now so it's on your phone. it's a working call: bring your last 30 days of numbers and we'll go through them live. if the time stops working, the reschedule link is in the invite.
24h before · email tomorrow, [time]. it's a working call, not a pitch: bring your last 30 days of numbers and we'll go through them live. calendar file attached.
3h before · sms we're on at [time] today. link: [link]. if the day blew up, reply 2 and you'll get two reschedule options, no hard feelings.
15min before · sms grabbing coffee, see you in 15. [link]
if they replied 2 · sms all good. two slots still open this week: [option a] and [option b]. reply with one and you're moved.

why it holds

the 24h email re-sells the value of showing up. the 3h text gives an honest exit ramp, which saves the lead who would otherwise just ghost. the 15min text is a human being, and it's the one people actually reply to. keep the voice yours: these read like texts from a person because that's the whole trick.

2

when they don't show

a no-show is a warm lead with bad calendar hygiene. the rebook message goes out the same hour, every time, no judgment in it. setters who guilt-trip no-shows lose them twice.

10 minutes past start · sms no stress, days blow up. i held the room 15 then gave it up. two slots left this week: [option a] and [option b]. reply with one and you're back in.
next day, if silent · dm still want eyes on your numbers? one reply and i'll re-open a slot. if now's wrong, say so and i'll leave it with you.

after that, stop. mark them and restart the thread fresh at the top of next month. a third chase in the same week reads desperate and poisons the list.

3

the booking flow behind it

messages patch a leak. the flow stops it. three fixes, each usually worth more than a new ad campaign.

a · qualify before the calendar

two questions on the booking form, before any times show: what the business is doing monthly, and how soon they want this fixed. wrong answers never reach your calendar, and the ones who reach it already invested thirty seconds, which is the first commitment of many.

b · the calendar file rides in the first email

the .ics attachment in the instant confirmation. a call that lives in their calendar with an alert is a different object from a link in an inbox. this is the cheapest fix on this page and the one most funnels skip.

c · the no-show rebook runs automatic

the two messages in section 2, wired to fire on the no-show event. nobody remembers to chase no-shows manually on a busy day, so the day it matters most is the day it gets skipped. automation exists for exactly this message.
