## The shape (locked 2026-08-20, do not renegotiate per prospect)

- **Flywheel on desktop, ordered stack on phone.** Offer at the hub, three engines on the ring
  (Attention, Sales, Delivery), Operations as the outer ring. Guided 1-2-3 badges point at the

- **IG DM is the only ask.** No WhatsApp number on the page; that swap happens in conversation
  after a reply.
- **Proof line:** consistent $50k months with a three person team. The Stripe slot stays
  "being added" until the real Apr-Aug capture exists. Never fake it, never predate it.

## The rules that are not style choices

- **Never invent a number, a quote, or a result.** If it was not observed, it does not ship.
  This killed a whole asset on 2026-08-23; it will not be relearned.
- **Their data, our proof.** Everything about THEM comes from public pulls made that day.
  Everything about US (the proof block, the realshot, the $50k line) is constant and already
  vetted. Do not mix the two.
- **Arman reviews every instance before it is sent.** The template makes the build fast;
  it does not remove the review.
